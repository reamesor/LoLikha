Designing Brands for the Digital-First Era

Oliver Hayes June 2, 2026 Branding

Identity that moves

Digital-first brands can’t treat the logo as the whole story. Identity now lives in motion, UI states, social crops, and product moments that change by the hour.

Systems over assets

The strongest brands ship flexible systems: type scales, color logic, component rules, and voice guidelines that teams can apply without waiting on a hero file from the brand book.

Behavior is branding

How a product feels — latency, empty states, error copy — shapes brand perception as much as a campaign. Designing for digital means designing for those moments on purpose.
