Crafting Interfaces People Love to Use

Sophie Nguyen June 9, 2026 Design

Love is low friction

People rarely say they love an interface because of a gradient. They love it because it respects their time, memory, and attention.

Microcopy as product design

Labels, empty states, and confirmations are part of the UI surface. Clear language reduces cognitive load more reliably than another icon set.

Beauty with purpose

Visual polish should guide the eye toward meaning. When aesthetics and usability pull in the same direction, products feel inevitable.
