Motion as a Language in Modern Interfaces

Kenji Tanaka June 16, 2026 Design

Motion means something

Animation isn’t decoration when it communicates state change, hierarchy, or spatial relationships. Used well, it teaches the product.

Timing is tone

Easing and duration shape personality. A 120ms snap feels precise; a 400ms ease can feel warm — or sluggish if misapplied.

Respect reduced motion

Inclusive motion design means offering calm alternatives. The message should survive even when the movement doesn’t.
