Design Systems That Scale With Your Team

Mark Otto June 8, 2026 Design

Systems are social contracts

A design system only works if people trust it. Documentation, contribution paths, and governance matter as much as the components themselves.

Start with the decisions you repeat

You don’t need every token on day one. Codify the patterns your team already argues about — spacing, type, buttons, forms — and grow from use.

Speed without sameness

Good systems accelerate production while leaving room for expressive moments. Consistency should free creativity, not flatten it.
